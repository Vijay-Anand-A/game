About Us

A Legacy of Melody & Discipline

Founded with the vision of preserving our musical heritage, Anandhavruksham Music Academy blends traditional gurukula values with a modern, encouraging learning environment.

  • ✓Expert Gurus — experienced performers and dedicated teachers guiding every student.
  • ✓Structured Curriculum — graded syllabus from fundamentals to advanced concert repertoire.
  • ✓Stage Exposure — regular concerts, arangetram and competitions to build confidence.

“Music is the soul of life — we teach it with devotion, patience and joy.”

From a child's first swara to a student's first solo performance, we walk every step together. Small batches, personal attention and a warm family atmosphere make learning here a truly memorable journey.
